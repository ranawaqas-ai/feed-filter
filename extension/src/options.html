Connection

This browser sends each video's title, channel, length and view count with your goal to Jev (api.typesafe.ai), using your key. A second request per video sends its title, channel and length to find its kind and whether the title is clickbait.

Feed topics

Optional. With an Anthropic key, the dashboard shows what YouTube is feeding you by topic. Once a week this browser sends the titles and channel names of videos YouTube offered you and videos you watched to Claude (api.anthropic.com), which groups them. Your goals are not sent.

From console.anthropic.com. Stored only in this browser profile and sent only to api.anthropic.com. One request a week, billed to your Anthropic account. Leave empty to turn the feature off.
