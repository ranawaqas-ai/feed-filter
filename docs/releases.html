Feed Filter

Releases

What each version of Feed Filter added, newest first. To update, unzip the new release over the same folder and click reload on the extension's card in Chrome.

0.5.0 19 November 2026

  • A video in the grey area now asks On goal? on its thumbnail, with Yes and No buttons that are always visible. Before, keeping or hiding it meant opening a three-dot menu that only showed on hover.
  • The match score and goal show in a small chip on the thumbnail while the pointer is on a card, above YouTube's own preview.
  • The dashboard lists of on-goal videos, near misses and your corrections show five rows each, with a button for the rest.
  • Feed topics you already turned into a goal or marked as not for you leave the topic list. They are kept under Decided earlier.
Download 0.5.0 on GitHub

0.4.0 16 November 2026

  • A setup page opens on first run. Two questions draft your first goal, and you paste your key and set a daily call limit there.
  • After setup, a panel on YouTube reports how many videos on your home feed serve the goal.
Download 0.4.0 on GitHub

0.3.0 16 November 2026

  • An optional dashboard card groups your feed into topics and shows which ones none of your goals cover. It needs an Anthropic key and is off until you add one.
  • A topic can be turned into a goal or an exclusion from that card.
Download 0.3.0 on GitHub

0.2.0 16 November 2026

  • The first public release. Videos are scored with your own Jev key, straight from the browser.
  • The extension comes as a zip to load in Chrome, with an install guide.
Download 0.2.0 on GitHub
